Church Device Control v0.4.1

Restore mixed-version settings sync: the stable version-1 envelope carries optional capabilities and additive settings, with a compatibility view for v0.4.0's version-2 reader. Names, rooms, addresses and deletions can be exchanged with older 0.3.x installations during staged upgrades. A 0.4.0/0.3.x pair still needs a 0.4.1 peer online or one member upgraded to bridge their original format mismatch.

Preserve unknown optional device fields across load/save, discovery and sync, including when a legacy peer strips them from an edit. Disabled has an independent revision so common-field edits cannot silently clear it. Compatibility tests exercise frozen v0.3 and v0.4 readers, both packet delivery orders, repeated legacy echoes, stale feature changes and future unknown fields.

Older software cannot implement new behavior: v0.3.x copies still do not understand Disabled and may refresh/control those devices until upgraded. This update keeps common settings compatible; it does not retrofit the old application's control rules.

Both application and installer are signed. Devices, room assignments, credentials and settings are preserved. Same sync key and LAN/firewall requirements as before.
